X2D stands for Exploit to Defend. You learn how an attack works, so you know how to shut it down.

Why it's built this way

Many cybersecurity courses stay theoretical, and you finish having memorized tool names without running a single attack. Here it's the opposite: every week has a Lab and a deliverable, every level ends with a practical exam, and the finish line is proof you can hand to an employer.

Who builds it with you

The instructor teaches from their own Penetration Testing experience, and shows you the mistakes they made themselves so you don't repeat them. It's not a copy of another course.

Here's what a week looks like

A steady rhythm: two live sessions, hands-on work in between, and a deliverable at the end.

  1. Monday, a 2-hour live session

    A new concept, applied in front of you on the terminal or the VM.

  2. Between sessions, Labs

    Specific tasks on TryHackMe or Hack The Box, with the instructor available on Discord.

  3. Friday, a 2-hour live session

    Deeper application, and the week's questions solved on screen.

  4. The deliverable

    A report, writeup, or script, with a clear deadline known from day one.

Questions people ask before signing up

Nothing is hidden. If your question isn't here, contact us.

I'm a complete beginner. Is that okay?
Yes, the program is designed for beginners. You don't need an IT background, just basic computer skills. We start with operating systems and the terminal before touching anything security-related.
Do I need prior cybersecurity experience?
No. What you need is to study and do the Labs seriously over 6 months. If you're looking for a quick certificate without the work, this course isn't right for you.
How do the sessions work?
Live on Discord with voice and screen sharing, on Monday and Friday, 2 hours each time. You see the terminal and the VM in front of you, and you practice along with us in real time.
What equipment do I need?
A computer or laptop with at least 8GB RAM, so you can run Kali and Windows as Virtual Machines side by side. TryHackMe and Hack The Box subscriptions are paid separately.
Is there a certificate?
Yes. When you finish the full 26-week track and pass the practical exams, you receive an X2D Academy certificate with a unique ID and a QR code. It's issued once, at the end of the full track, not per level. You also leave with a complete Pentest report and a project portfolio.
How does someone verify my certificate?
They scan the QR code on the certificate, or enter its ID on the verification page. No account needed. The page shows the certificate details, what the program covered, and the exams you passed.
What kind of certificate is it?
It's an X2D Academy certificate of completion, not a university degree or a vendor exam like CompTIA or OSCP. What makes it useful is that it's verifiable: anyone can scan it and see exactly what you completed, and you can back it up with the Pentest report you built.
How is it different from a recorded course?
Here there's someone in front of you answering when you get stuck, following up with you on Discord all week. The course is live and built on the instructor's real experience, not just videos.
How much time will I need?
The sessions are 4 live hours a week, and that's just the start: every week has a Lab and a deliverable. Plan for a fixed practice time, especially in the Subnetting and Active Directory weeks because they're the hardest.
What if I don't pass the practical exam?
We sit down together and pinpoint exactly which part needs redoing, and set another assessment date. The next level doesn't unlock until you pass.
I have experience. Can I start at a higher level?
Possibly. We give you a placement test, and if you pass it you start from the next level.
